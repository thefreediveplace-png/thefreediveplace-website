WALEED GHATAS · DAHAB

EXPERIENCE, WITH HUMILITY

Meet Waleed
Ghatas.

Sharing the freedom and focus of freediving, one student and one breath at a time.

Waleed began freediving in Dahab in 2012 and started teaching in 2015. As an SSI Freediving Instructor Trainer, he has certified hundreds of recreational and professional students and coached freedivers, including national record-holding athletes.

He is also an AIDA Level E judge and CMAS International Judge.
